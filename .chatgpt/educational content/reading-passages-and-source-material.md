# Reading passages and source material

Reading passages, dialogues, notices and other source materials are part of the construct being assessed. They must be accurate, level-appropriate, fair and legally usable.

## Required record

For each source text, record:

- Stable source identifier and version.
- Full learner-facing text and its language variant.
- Intended CEFR level and a rationale for that level.
- Reading purpose and skills targeted (for example locating information, main idea, detail, inference or argument).
- Author or original source, publication details and URL where applicable.
- Rights status, licence or permission evidence.
- Whether the text is original, quoted, adapted, abridged or simplified.
- A summary of every material adaptation, simplification or translation.
- Date accessed/created and responsible editor.
- Associated questions and the evidence in the text supporting each answer.

## Educational requirements

A passage must:

1. Be coherent, natural and factually accurate where it makes factual claims.
2. Match the target level in vocabulary, syntax, cohesion, length and inferential demand.
3. Provide sufficient evidence for every associated correct answer.
4. Avoid requiring outside knowledge unless that knowledge is explicitly part of the learning objective.
5. Avoid ambiguity caused by pronouns, references, missing context or contradictory details.
6. Use inclusive, age-appropriate and culturally fair subject matter for the intended audience.
7. Avoid unnecessary sensitive content or personal-data collection.
8. Preserve the intended meaning when simplified, abridged or adapted.
9. Be readable and accessible in the actual learner interface.

Questions must distinguish what the passage states from what a learner may infer. Inference questions need a defensible evidence trail; they must not reward guessing the author's unstated intention.

## Copyright and provenance

Do not copy substantial text from books, articles, websites or other protected sources without a suitable licence or permission. A URL alone is not evidence of permission to reproduce.

For adapted material, retain the original source, rights basis and a description of changes. If rights are unclear, do not publish the material.

## Review requirements

A qualified teacher must review linguistic quality, level fit, question-to-passage alignment and answer evidence. An editor must verify source attribution, rights, adaptation notes and accessibility.

## Release blockers

Unclear rights, missing provenance, inaccurate or distorted adaptation, unsupported answers, level mismatch or material ambiguity blocks publication.
