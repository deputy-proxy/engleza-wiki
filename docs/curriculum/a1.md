# A1 learning path: Everyday English Foundations

**CEFR target:** A1  
**Entry assumption:** The learner can recognise a few familiar words and phrases but needs substantial support.  
**Exit outcome:** The learner can understand and use familiar everyday expressions, introduce themselves, ask and answer simple questions, and interact in a basic way when the other person speaks slowly and clearly.  
**Path structure:** 5 units × 4 lessons; one reviewed unit checkpoint per unit.

## Unit 1 — Me, my family and introductions

**Unit outcome:** Exchange basic personal information and understand simple questions about identity and family.

| Order | Lesson | Measurable objective | Target language and skill |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Greetings and spelling names | Greet someone, say goodbye and spell a name using the English alphabet. | Greetings, alphabet, classroom phrases; listening and speaking |
| 2 | Introducing yourself | Give your name, country and one simple personal detail in a short introduction. | I am / My name is / I'm from; be, subject pronouns |
| 3 | Family and people | Identify close family members and describe who someone is. | Family nouns, possessive adjectives, this/that |
| 4 | Asking for personal information | Ask and answer simple questions about name, origin, age and family. | Wh-questions, yes/no questions with be; guided dialogue |

**Checkpoint:** Short listening and reading items plus a supported personal-information exchange. Assess comprehension and intelligibility, not accent imitation.

## Unit 2 — Everyday objects, places and descriptions

**Unit outcome:** Identify common objects and places and give very simple descriptions.

| Order | Lesson | Measurable objective | Target language and skill |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Things around you | Name common classroom, home and personal objects. | Everyday nouns, a/an, plural -s |
| 2 | Describing objects | Describe an object using a basic adjective and noun phrase. | Colours, size, common adjectives, adjective order at phrase level |
| 3 | Where things are | Ask and say where familiar objects are located. | in/on/under/next to, there is/there are |
| 4 | Describing your home | Give a short, supported description of rooms and familiar furniture. | Rooms, furniture, singular/plural, simple prepositions |

**Checkpoint:** Label and locate objects from a simple picture; answer short questions about a familiar room.

## Unit 3 — Routines, time and everyday life

**Unit outcome:** Describe a simple daily routine and understand basic times and schedules.

| Order | Lesson | Measurable objective | Target language and skill |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Days, dates and clock time | Understand and state days, common dates and times. | Days, months, numbers, at/on for basic time expressions |
| 2 | Daily activities | Describe a small set of everyday routines. | High-frequency verbs, present simple affirmative |
| 3 | Asking about routines | Ask and answer simple questions about habits and schedules. | Present simple questions/negatives with do; frequency basics |
| 4 | A typical day | Write and say a short sequence of routine sentences. | Sequencing with first, then, after that; time expressions |

**Checkpoint:** Understand a simple timetable and produce a short, comprehensible routine description.

## Unit 4 — Food, shopping and everyday needs

**Unit outcome:** Identify common food and make simple requests in predictable transactions.

| Order | Lesson | Measurable objective | Target language and skill |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Food and drink | Name common food and drink and express basic likes. | Food vocabulary, countable/uncountable awareness, like |
| 2 | Asking for what you need | Make a simple request politely. | I'd like / Can I have / please / thank you |
| 3 | Numbers, prices and quantities | Ask and understand simple prices and quantities. | Numbers, currency, How much, basic quantity phrases |
| 4 | At a café or shop | Complete a short, rehearsed transaction with support. | Functional dialogue, listening for price/item/quantity |

**Checkpoint:** Role-play a short café/shop exchange and interpret a simple menu or price list.

## Unit 5 — Getting around and bringing it together

**Unit outcome:** Ask for and understand very simple directions and combine familiar language in short exchanges.

| Order | Lesson | Measurable objective | Target language and skill |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Places in town | Identify common public places and services. | Town vocabulary, a/an/the in familiar phrases |
| 2 | Asking where places are | Ask where a place is and understand a short answer. | Where is, basic location prepositions |
| 3 | Simple directions | Follow and give a few basic directions with visual support. | go straight, turn left/right, imperatives |
| 4 | My local area | Give a short description of a familiar place and ask a simple follow-up question. | Recycled personal information, places, routines and directions |

**Checkpoint:** Integrated listening/reading plus a supported spoken or written task about the learner's local area.

## A1 assessment blueprint

Each unit checkpoint should sample its stated outcome using a mix of listening/reading comprehension and controlled language use. The final path review should sample all five unit outcomes. Keep productive tasks short and scaffolded; use familiar vocabulary and concrete contexts. Distractors must be plausible but unambiguous. Every scored item requires a teacher-verified answer key and explanation.
