# Editorial quality rubric

This rubric applies to every public resource, test, lesson, listening/reading activity, question, answer key and Romanian translation. A published database status is not evidence of editorial approval.

## Review outcomes

| Outcome | Meaning | Public behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| `approved` | Every applicable rubric item passes and the reviewer records approval | Publicly visible; indexable only when SEO controls allow it |
| `revise` | Useful material exists but one or more quality requirements fail | Hidden until revised and re-approved |
| `unpublish` | Incomplete, misleading, unusable or not fit for public learning | Draft/unpublished |
| `noindex` | Useful for learners but not suitable as a search landing page | Publicly visible with `noindex`; excluded from sitemap |
| `remove` | Duplicate, obsolete, rights-infringing or irreparable | Archived |
| `pending` | Not yet reviewed or review was invalidated by an edit | Hidden from public routes, search and sitemap |

A reviewer must record the outcome, reviewer account, timestamp and useful notes. Editing reviewed source content, an answer key, an interactive child record or a translation invalidates the relevant approval.

## Scored rubric

Score each applicable criterion from 0 to 2: **0 = fails/missing**, **1 = partial**, **2 = meets standard**. A public approval requires a score of 2 for accuracy, answer-key integrity, level fit, accessibility and rights, and no unresolved critical issue. Other criteria must average at least 1.7. A score never overrides a critical failure.

1. **Accuracy:** grammar, vocabulary, factual claims, spelling, punctuation and examples are correct.
2. **Learning objective:** the learner can tell what they will be able to understand or do after the activity.
3. **Explanation:** concepts are explained, not merely named; terminology is defined at the learner's level.
4. **Worked examples:** examples are natural, varied, contextualised and demonstrate the rule or target skill.
5. **Common mistakes:** likely learner errors are anticipated and corrected where useful.
6. **Practice:** exercises genuinely assess the objective, progress in difficulty and avoid ambiguous prompts.
7. **Answer key and feedback:** every objectively scored question has a correct, verified key; explanations explain why, not just repeat the answer.
8. **CEFR fit:** vocabulary, syntax, text length, task demand and cognitive load match the assigned level.
9. **Accessibility:** meaningful headings, readable structure, keyboard-operable controls, captions/transcripts where applicable and no meaning conveyed by colour alone.
10. **Romanian translation:** meaning, instruction and pedagogical intent are preserved; English target-language examples remain English where they teach English.
11. **Completeness:** all referenced media, questions, answer keys, metadata and links exist and work.
12. **SEO and rights:** title/description/canonical/indexing are intentional; media has documented source, licence/permission and attribution.

## Content-type checks

- **Grammar/article/guide:** objective, concise explanation, form and use, positive/negative/question patterns where relevant, at least three contextual examples, common mistakes, meaningful practice and explained answer key.
- **Vocabulary:** level-appropriate selection, part of speech, learner-friendly meaning, natural collocations, contextual examples and practice that tests meaning and use.
- **Worksheet/exercise:** instructions, objective, coherent progression, unambiguous prompts, complete key and explanations.
- **Flashcards:** one clear learning target per card, accurate definition/answer, no misleading simplification, appropriate level and useful context for ambiguous terms.
- **Listening:** playable media, transcript or equivalent accessible support, documented media rights, clear task purpose, questions answerable from the audio and validated answer keys.
- **Reading:** authentic or clearly labelled adapted text, appropriate length and vocabulary, coherent text structure, questions supported by the passage and validated keys.
- **Writing:** communicative purpose, audience/register, clear task constraints, useful planning guidance, word range/time where appropriate, task-specific rubric and a level-appropriate model response when it helps.
- **Tests:** blueprint aligned to level and skill, balanced coverage, reliable difficulty, no duplicated/ambiguous items, valid options and teacher-validated keys/explanations.
- **Lessons:** measurable objectives, CEFR/skill/topic alignment, purposeful activity order, prerequisites and complete referenced activities.
- **Translations:** source hash current, correct terminology and diacritics, no machine-literal phrasing that changes meaning, and a verified source-language fallback.

## Review evidence

The reviewer should record a short rationale, particularly for answer-key corrections, CEFR changes, rights decisions and noindex/unpublish outcomes. Core grammar, CEFR placement and assessment answer keys require a qualified English-language teacher. High-impact grammar/CEFR content requires a second independent review.
