# Editorial reviewer responsibilities

## Author

- Supplies accurate source material, a clear learning objective, CEFR/skill/topic metadata and complete learner instructions.
- Verifies that referenced assets and links exist and that media rights are documented.
- Responds to revision notes and requests another review after changes.

## English-language teacher reviewer

- Verifies grammar/vocabulary accuracy, naturalness, examples, practice quality, answer keys and explanations.
- Confirms CEFR level and skill/topic alignment against the CEFR standards and curriculum objective.
- Reviews Romanian support text for pedagogical meaning while preserving English learning material.
- Records a decision and concise evidence in the CMS.

## Second reviewer

- Independently reviews core grammar explanations, high-impact CEFR assignments and consequential assessment keys.
- Resolves disagreements with the primary reviewer before approval.

## Editor/publisher

- Checks completeness, accessibility, internal links, metadata, canonical/indexing behaviour and content rights.
- Confirms the required teacher reviews are recorded before approval.
- Uses unpublish/noindex/remove when quality or rights issues cannot be resolved immediately.

A developer or automated audit can identify missing fields, structural defects and likely placeholders. It cannot substitute for qualified teacher validation or grant itself teacher approval.
