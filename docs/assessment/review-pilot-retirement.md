# Assessment review, pilot and retirement procedure

## Before a form can be published

1. Complete the blueprint and item-bank audit with no blocking findings.
2. Have a qualified English-language teacher review every item, key, explanation, distractor and level rationale.
3. Obtain a second independent teacher review for core grammar and high-impact placement items.
4. Have the editor confirm review evidence, translations, accessibility and source/rights metadata.
5. Re-run the automated bank audit after every content change.

## Pilot protocol

1. Define the intended learner population, recruitment criteria, consent/privacy handling and the decision the test is meant to support.
2. Pilot each level form with learners spanning the intended level and adjacent levels. Record the sample size, cohort characteristics, administration conditions and completion/abandonment rates.
3. Do not infer psychometric validity from a handful of convenience responses. Set a sample-size target and analysis plan with an assessment specialist before collecting responses.
4. Where sample size supports it, calculate item difficulty, discrimination, distractor performance, internal consistency and form-level score distributions. Document confidence/uncertainty and subgroup limitations.
5. Investigate items with ambiguous responses, non-functioning distractors, extreme difficulty or weak discrimination. Teacher-review revisions and pilot them again.
6. Compare the resulting estimates with an independent placement measure or qualified teacher judgement. Do not describe this as CEFR validation unless the design and evidence support that claim.

## Retirement and exposure control

- Retire items that are inaccurate, ambiguous, exposed too widely, rights-restricted or persistently non-discriminating.
- Keep a versioned record of the item, decision, reason and replacement. Do not rewrite historical attempt snapshots or recalculate completed scores.
- Maintain separate pools for formal assessment and adaptive practice. Do not leak formal answer keys into learner payloads before completion.
- Rotate equivalent forms only after their blueprint and review requirements are satisfied; record the form/version used by each attempt.

## Current pilot status

No representative learner pilot or psychometric validation has been completed for the current demo bank. There are no defensible item difficulty/discrimination statistics to report. Until a pilot and independent comparison are complete, results remain indicative practice estimates only.
