# What the English level tests measure

The A1-B2 level tests are short, free practice assessments that sample grammar, vocabulary and reading comprehension. They provide an **indicative estimate** to help learners choose what to practise next.

They do not assess listening, speaking or writing, and they are not official CEFR examinations, certificates or proof of language proficiency. A result should be interpreted alongside other evidence, such as a teacher's assessment and the learner's performance in real communication.

The current demo question bank has not completed qualified teacher review or representative learner piloting. Its results must not be treated as validated placement decisions.
